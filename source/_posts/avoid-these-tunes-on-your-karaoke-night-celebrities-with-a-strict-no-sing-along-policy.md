## Avoid These Tunes on Your Karaoke Night: Celebrities With a Strict No-Sing Along Policy!

[![](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/karaokenoflylist1.jpg?resize=300%2C270&ssl=1)](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/karaokenoflylist1.jpg?fit=300%2C300&ssl=1 "karaokenoflylist")

****UPDATED 8-10-17 | Songwriters That Don’t Want You To Sing Their Songs in the United States of America**

**The** **karaoke “No-Fly” list is a real thing.** These original Artists do not want you to sing their songs, and **US law dictates that you need explicit permission from the artist to produce and sell their song in karaoke format.** It’s a shame. It’s as if **these Artists have really lost touch with what drew them to music in the first place** and who made them who they are…why not let your fans honor with emulation? As they say, that’s the highest form of flattery…

The US and the UK are the only countries in the world with a copyright law that prevents karaoke companies from producing songs without special permission from the songwriter.
