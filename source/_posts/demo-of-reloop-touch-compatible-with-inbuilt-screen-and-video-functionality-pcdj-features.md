## Demo of Reloop Touch - Compatible with Inbuilt Screen & Video Functionality | PCDJ Features

In the video above we demonstrate DEX 3 display support for the [Reloop DJ Touch controller](https://www.reloop.com/reloop-touch).

The Reloop Touch DJ Controller has a built-in native touch-screen display. The upcoming DEX 3.14 release (which may be available at the time you’re reviewing this blog post) will include native support for the Reloop Touch and the built-in display.

As shown in the video, we’ve completely customized a ‘native display’ skin to make better use of the provided real estate that includes just what you need to see, and nothing that you don’t.

The Reloop Touch is the first DJ controller where [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) supports the large built-in display. Now that support is in place we can support similar controllers with native displays in the future. As it becomes more commonplace for DJ controllers to include large built-in displays, touch screen or otherwise, we’ll be ready!

If you haven’t yet tried DEX 3, our true all-in-one live performance DJ software, [download it it for a test spin](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) today!
