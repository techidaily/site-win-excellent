### The Laptop/Computer Specifications

First, you’re going to need a proper laptop, either MAC or Windows, that’s capable of running karaoke software efficiently and problem free. Here are the minimum system requirements we recommend:

**Mac Requirements:**

* OSX 10.9 or greater
* 2.5GHz Intel Core 2 Duo / Core 2 Quad / Core i3/i5/i7
* 4 GIG RAM or Better
* Video card: Dedicated Video Card or Intel 3000 series (or Iris) or better
* 200 MB free on the hard-drive

**Windows Requirements:**

* Windows 7 / Windows 8 / Windows 8.1 / Windows 10
* 2.5GHz Intel Core 2 Duo / Core 2 Quad / Core i3/i5/i7 / Amd Phenon
* 4 GIG RAM or Better
* Video card: Intel HD 3000 Series Graphics Card or Better
* 200 MB free on the hard-drive

No one brand sticks out above the others, but you also don’t want bargain basement and otherwise entry level machines. You need something that can hold up to the rigors of weekly abuse. If you allow smoking in your venue, especially.

### The Karaoke Hardware

The various hardware-based karaoke equipment you’ll need can be sourced from stores such as Guitar Center or online avenues such as [sweetwater.com](https://www.sweetwater.com/) or [musiciansfriend.com](https://www.musiciansfriend.com/). If you have a local pro audio dealer you prefer than go with them.

Here’s a rundown of what essential hardware is required for your karaoke bar or venue — print this list and take it your local pro audio retailer or find online.

* Karaoke Mixer/Amp (PA style mixing board with at least 3 mic inputs – one for the karaoke host, two for the singers (duets))
* Minimum of 2 Speakers with minimum 15″ sub-woofer
* 2 Speaker Stands for the two ‘tops’
* 2-3 Wireless Microphones (The KJ’s mic can be corded)
* RF Converter
* 19″ Color LCD/LED TV with minimum resolution of 1366×768 (or another size if you prefer – but 19″ is fairly standard for the main ‘singers display’ for the lyrics)
* TV stand (or mount)
* Component Rack (hardware to mount all the hardware gear, and the laptop for the karaoke host, in one ‘rack’ or table/stand)
* Various Cables and Accessories to connect the hardware components (including HDMI for the lyrics output).

With all these items combined you’ll be up and running with a **karaoke system for a bar** that meets today’s standards and ensures a quality, problem free, karaoke event.
